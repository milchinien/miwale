Game Style Library · open & free to copy

Art styles your AI can actually copy.

Most AIs are bad at inventing a coherent look for a game. So here are 20 finished, playable styles – each broken down into exact colours, fonts, rendering technique, characters, effects, UI and motion, with a step-by-step recipe and starter code that runs without dependencies.

How to use a style with an AI

  1. Pick a styleFilter by mood or genre, try the sandbox and the asset sheet.
  2. Copy the prompt“Copy prompt” bundles the complete spec and the starter code into one message.
  3. Add your gamePaste it into your AI, describe your game below it. The spec keeps the look consistent.

AI with web access? Just give it https://miwale.com/styles/llms.txt – it lists every style with direct links to the specs.

Built by miwale, a one-person game studio from Leipzig that makes games with AI. All styles were made for the game-jam prototype Jack vs. the Turnip Ancestors – same game, 20 completely different looks.

Free to copy and adapt for your own games. A link back to miwale.com/styles is appreciated.
