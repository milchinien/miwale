How to use a style with an AI

  1. Pick a style – or mix oneEvery style shows its six elements separately. In the mixer you combine elements of different styles.
  2. Copy the prompt“Copy prompt” bundles the complete spec and the starter code into one message.
  3. Describe your projectPaste it into your AI and describe your project in the marked spot. The spec keeps the look consistent.

AI with web access? Just give it https://miwale.com/styles/llms.txt – it lists every style with direct links to the specs.
