Aleator – sound effects

All sound effects are real recordings. Almost all are released under CC0 1.0 (public domain);
no attribution is required for those, credited here anyway with thanks. The telephone bell is CC BY 4.0 (see below).

Kenney (www.kenney.nl) – CC0
  Casino Audio      dice shakes and throws, chips, cards
  Impact Sounds     wood knocks, punches, plastic and plate knocks (phone handset)
  RPG Audio         coins, book pages, leather, metal click (phone hang-up)
  Interface Sounds  switch (phone hook)

Versilian Community Sample Library – VCSL (github.com/sgossner/VCSL) – CC0
  Marimba, vibraphone, glockenspiel, tubular bells, mark trees, bell tree,
  triangle, crash and suspended cymbals, gong, claves, sleigh bells,
  timpani, bass drum, concert harp, tenor saxophone, grand piano (Steinway B)

Telephone bell (phone_ring) – CC BY 4.0
  "WWS AutomatictelephoneIskraEta80ringing.ogg" by Work With Sounds / Technical Museum of Slovenia,
  Wikimedia Commons, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWS_AutomatictelephoneIskraEta80ringing.ogg
  License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (one ring burst cut out, normalised, converted to MP3)

The files in this folder are trimmed, normalised mono MP3s built by tools/build_sfx.py.
sfx-data.js holds the same MP3s as Base64 so the game also runs from file://.
