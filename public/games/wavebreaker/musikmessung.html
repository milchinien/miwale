Music, measured

Six questions, six numbers. Are the three moods really different? Does it click at the loop point? Does the change crossfade, or does it cut? Does anything still run with music at zero? What does it all cost per frame? And — the question this music once failed on — does the setup freeze the frame?

Everything runs against the same files as the game — src/app/musicplan.ts, src/app/musicbed.ts, src/app/mixer.ts, src/app/audio.ts. The sound scenes are computed in an OfflineAudioContext, faster than real time and without anyone having to listen. The bands are measured with real BiquadFilterNode band-pass filters rather than a hand-rolled Fourier transform: the self-test answers the same question with an FFT, and two different methods that agree are worth more than one method run twice.

Everything above runs in an OfflineAudioContext and therefore needs neither ears nor a click. Here the same question is asked again of the real engine: through mountAudio, with an AudioContext that the browser only unlocks after you interact with the page. In addition, the frame time with music playing is measured against the frame time with music at zero, over 240 frames each — this also catches what a pure offline calculation cannot see.
