Würfelglück – sound effects

All sound effects are real recordings released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).
No attribution is required; credited here anyway with thanks.

Kenney (www.kenney.nl) – CC0
  Casino Audio      dice shakes and throws, chips, cards
  Impact Sounds     wood knocks, punches
  RPG Audio         coins, book pages, leather

Versilian Community Sample Library – VCSL (github.com/sgossner/VCSL) – CC0
  Marimba, vibraphone, glockenspiel, tubular bells, mark trees, bell tree,
  triangle, crash and suspended cymbals, gong, claves, sleigh bells,
  timpani, bass drum, concert harp, tenor saxophone, grand piano (Steinway B)

The files in this folder are trimmed, normalised mono MP3s built by tools/build_sfx.py.
sfx-data.js holds the same MP3s as Base64 so the game also runs from file://.
