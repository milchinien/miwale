How to Play

WASD / Arrows move – Jack throws pumpkin seeds automatically
Space headbutt dash (briefly invulnerable)
1 · 2 · 3 pick an upgrade on level-up · R reroll
Esc / P pause · M mute

Every world is one evening: from afternoon through dusk into the night, the world transforms around you. At midnight the world’s boss awakens – each world has its own. Defeat him and a portal opens – and the next world unlocks.

You always keep the Rutabagas you collect. In World Select you spend them on permanent upgrades, improve single upgrades through Mastery and unlock rare card Rarities.
